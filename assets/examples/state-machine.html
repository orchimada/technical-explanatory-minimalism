The click pen, proven.

One push-button drives a pen that toggles. The trick is a rotating cam: every release rotates it one notch in the same direction, but the notches alternate between deep (tip in) and shallow (tip out). Step through it and the diagram proves the four-state cycle — no hand-waving.
