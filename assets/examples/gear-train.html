The gear train.

Two meshing gears trade speed for the same force. The smaller driven gear turns faster than the larger driver — by exactly the ratio of their tooth counts. Drag either gear to feel the coupling; the readout shows the live ratio.

Method: figure/ground separation · Not to scale
